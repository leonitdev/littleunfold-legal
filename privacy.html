Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Little Unfold is a calm puzzle game. We built it to work without collecting any information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Little Unfold has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data, and it does not send any data to us or to third parties.

What stays on your device

Your settings, the levels you have finished and your in-progress level are saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Erase everything, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock all levels" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock the levels. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Little Unfold does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
