Support

Need help with Little Unfold? Email hello@foveo.tech and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Each level has three steps. Stroke along the ribbons to straighten them. Drag each loose piece into its spot. Then tap the closed bud, shell, stone or egg until it opens.

I'm stuck on a level

Use the helper button at the bottom of the screen: it straightens a ribbon or shows which piece to move. In Settings you can also turn on tile markings that show where every piece belongs.

I bought the full game. How do I restore it?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID you bought it with. On a new device, purchases restore automatically.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Turning off sound or vibration

Open Settings in the app. Sounds, background music and haptics each have their own switch.
